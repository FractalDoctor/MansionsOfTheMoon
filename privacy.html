Mansions of the Moon — Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

Data Collection

Mansions of the Moon does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. The app does not require an account, registration, or sign-in of any kind.

Network Requests

Mansions of the Moon does not make any network requests. The app functions entirely offline. All astronomical calculations are performed locally on your device. There is no server communication of any kind.

Local Data Storage

Mansions of the Moon stores no user data on device. All content is bundled with the app and all calculations are performed on demand. Nothing is persisted between sessions.

Location Services

Mansions of the Moon does not request or use location services. The lunar mansion system is based on the Moon’s ecliptic longitude, which is the same for all observers on Earth at any given moment.

Analytics and Tracking

Mansions of the Moon does not include any analytics frameworks, crash reporters, advertising SDKs, or third-party tracking of any kind.

Third-Party Services

Mansions of the Moon does not communicate with any third-party services.

Children’s Privacy

Mansions of the Moon does not collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a revised date.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please open an issue on the Mansions of the Moon GitHub repository.
